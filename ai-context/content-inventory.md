## Translation Notes

### General Guidelines

1. **Preserve meaning and tone** - not literal word-for-word translation
2. **Keep technical terms** - TypeScript, Node.js, etc. may remain in English
3. **Maintain professional tone** - formal but not overly formal Hebrew
4. **Avoid idioms** - use clear, direct language that translates well
5. **Shorter sentences** - Hebrew can be more concise
6. **RTL considerations** - ensure layout works with right-to-left text

### Technical Terms to Consider

- Some terms may remain in English: TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, GraphQL, REST, Oracle, MSSQL, MongoDB, CRMs
- Consider Hebrew alternatives for: "backend", "full-stack", "refactoring" (if natural Hebrew exists)
- Test with native Hebrew speakers for natural phrasing
