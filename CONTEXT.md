# Avi Koenig Portfolio (v3)

A personal portfolio that presents Avi Koenig as a senior backend-oriented full-stack developer who safely evolves existing production and legacy systems. It speaks to two audiences: recruiters (home, Case Studies, Mindset, Resume) and prospective clients (Services). This glossary pins down the language the site and its content use.

## Language

**Case Study**:
A concrete, anonymized account of a real engagement, structured as Situation → Approach → Outcome → Stack. The site's primary proof-of-work unit.
_Avoid_: Project, Experience Pattern, Portfolio Item

**Service**:
An offering presented to prospective clients (e.g. integrations, production software, AI over business data), paired with a "Delivered" proof point and backed by a Case Study where one exists. Services may name organizations; Case Studies may not.
_Avoid_: Product, Package, Offer

**Mindset**:
The abstract narrative of how Avi works — his engineering values and working style, independent of any specific engagement.
_Avoid_: How I Work, Philosophy, Experience (as a section)

**Anonymized**:
A property of Case Study content: real engagements are described without client names or identifying specifics, focused on the situation and the work rather than the who.
_Avoid_: Redacted, Generic
